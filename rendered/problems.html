How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the shared parser reads every date month first, so day and month swap. It happens in the app, in email and in booking-v2 forms.
  • hint: the tenant runs booking-v3. Its form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people type the month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Day and month swap here too, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint is the bug.

One report shows a different day in Lisbon and Toronto for the same meeting, one has an invite that arrived before its booking, and one says only June is off.
